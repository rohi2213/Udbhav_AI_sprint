Transport and Commute

No fares or routes are listed because none are verified. Check current options locally.

City bus

Ask your landlord or colleagues which routes serve your locality; check timings and stops.

Auto-rickshaw / e-rickshaw

Agree on the fare before riding, or insist on the meter where available.

Ride-hailing apps

Compare fares across apps for your usual route and time of day.

Own vehicle or cycle

Include fuel, parking and maintenance in your monthly estimate.

Shared rides

Colleagues or classmates on the same route may share costs; meet in public places first.
